Gelato

Gelato is Italy’s legendary frozen dessert, cherished for its rich flavor, velvety texture, and artisanal roots. Unlike traditional ice cream, gelato is made with more milk and less cream, and churned at a slower speed — resulting in a denser, smoother scoop that’s lower in fat but bursting with taste. It’s served slightly warmer than ice cream, allowing its flavors to shine through with every silky bite.

Bingsu

Bingsu is a beloved Korean shaved ice dessert made from ultra-fine milk ice, creating a snow-like texture that melts in your mouth. It's typically topped with a variety of sweet ingredients — from fresh fruits and syrups to red beans, mochi, and ice cream. Each bowl is a refreshing, customizable treat perfect for hot days or indulgent cravings.

Popsicle

Ice pops are frozen treats made by blending fruit, juice, milk, or other flavorful ingredients and freezing them into handheld molds. Unlike ice cream, they’re lighter, often dairy-free, and come in vibrant, refreshing flavors. From tropical blends like pineapple banana to tangy favorites like mango lime, ice pops are the ultimate cool-down snack — easy to make, fun to eat, and endlessly customizable.
