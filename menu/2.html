CREPES

Delicately thin and irresistibly tender, French crêpes are the epitome of culinary elegance. Crafted from a whisper-light batter of eggs, flour, milk, and butter, they cook to a golden perfection with lacy edges and a soft, pliable center. Whether folded around sweet fillings like fresh berries and whipped cream or rolled with savory delights such as ham and Gruyère, crêpes offer a versatile canvas for indulgence. Their subtle flavor and melt-in-your-mouth texture make them a timeless classic—perfect for breakfast, brunch, or a refined dessert.
