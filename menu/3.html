PATBINGSU

Patbingsu (빙수) is Korea’s iconic shaved ice dessert, a delightful blend of tradition and refreshment. At its heart lies a fluffy mound of ultra-fine ice—soft as fresh powder—topped with sweet red bean paste (pat) that’s been simmered to a rich, velvety texture. Often adorned with chewy rice cakes, condensed milk, fresh fruit, or a scoop of ice cream, patbingsu offers a playful mix of flavors and textures in every spoonful. Cool, creamy, and subtly sweet, it’s the perfect way to beat the heat while savoring a taste of Korean nostalgia.
