POPSICLES

Ingredients

Pineapple Banana Ice Pops

  • 2 cups (500 mL) cubed fresh pineapple
  • 1 small banana
  • 1 cup (250 mL) Milk
  • 1 1/2 tsp (7 mL) vanilla
  • Generous pinch of nutmeg

Honey Strawberry Ice Pops

  • 3 cups (750 mL) chopped strawberries
  • 1/4 cup (60 mL) honey
  • 2 tbsp (30 mL) lemon juice
  • 1 cup (250 mL) Milk

Mango Lime Ice Pops

  • 3 cups (750 mL) frozen mango pieces
  • 1/4 cup (60 mL) granulated sugar
  • 1 lime
  • 1 cup (250 mL) Milk

Preparation

Pineapple Banana Ice Pops

Place all ingredients in a blender. Purée until smooth. Pour into 8 ice pop moulds. Freeze at least 4 hours.

Honey Strawberry Ice Pops

Place strawberries, honey and lemon juice in a blender. Pulse a couple of times to mix. Add milk and purée until smooth. Pour into 8 ice pop moulds. Freeze at least 4 hours.

Mango Lime Ice Pops

Place mangoes, sugar, zest and juice of lime in a blender. Pulse a couple of times to break up mango. Add milk and purée until smooth. Even if mango is still slightly frozen, spoon into 8 ice pop moulds. Freeze at least 4 hours.
